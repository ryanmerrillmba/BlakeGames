Monster Field Guide

Here is how to defeat the bad guys from your drawings!

Poster (World 1 Boss)

Behavior: A giant grey face that hovers, locks onto you, and slams down to the floor!

How to Beat: 🏃 Move away to avoid getting squashed! Once he lands flat and is smashed on the floor, run up and touch him to ⚡️ electrify and damage him!

●

Nippins

Behavior: Purple rolling balls that bounce and waddle through the early levels.

How to Beat: Jump on a Nippin's head or zap it before it rolls into you.

Star-Shell Bug

Behavior: Crawls slowly, protected by a shell with a red star.

How to Beat: Jump on its head to squash it, or zap it!

♛
● ● ● ●

Nippin King (World 1 Boss)

Behavior: A crowned purple Nippin who rules a dungeon full of bouncing Nippins.

How to Beat: Jump on the king when he drops low, and dodge the Nippins protecting his dungeon.

⚡️💥

Spark-Ball

Behavior: Spawns when electric zaps hit a platform. Crawls quickly back and forth.

How to Beat: Jump on its head to squash it or avoid its crackling spikes.

Blood Shark

Behavior: Hops like a bouncy electric creature and shoots bouncing zaps. He can also fire a horizontal stream of lasers!

How to Beat: Jump on his head when he is not whacking, or use a box to reflect his laser.

Dragon Wing

Behavior: Flies and spits out yellow zaps that hit platforms and turn into spark-balls!

How to Beat: Jump on its head or dodge the zaps from below.

💧

Gray Pond

Behavior: Murky, slow-sinking water in World 2-1. Submerged enemies move slowly along the bottom.

How to Navigate: Press and hold UP to swim upward and leap out of the water!

🦈

Shark Fin Hazard

Behavior: A wiggling gray fin swimming back and forth across the Gray Pond's surface.

How to Avoid: Dodge the fin! Touching it deals 1 damage and pops the player high into the air.

Dungeon Emperor (Final Boss)

Behavior: Spawns in World 5-3. Combines ground slams, horizontal lasers, and triple electric zaps!

How to Beat: Dodge his lasers and zaps. When he slams flat on the ground, touch him to damage him. Defeat him to win the game!

Keyboard Controls

🚶 Move Left / Right: Left/Right Arrows (or A/D)

🦘 Jump: Up Arrow (or W / Space)

💥 Ground Pound: Down Arrow (or S) while in mid-air!

🥷 Sneak (Hip-P only): Hold Down Arrow on the ground to shrink!

⛏️ Dig (Tuskanty only): Hold Down Arrow while standing on brown dirt blocks!

📦 Box Bounce (Hosco only): Jump, then press Up Arrow again in mid-air!

How to Beat the Boss (Nippin King) 👑

1. 🟣 Clear the purple Nippins from the dungeon!

2. 🦘 Stomp the Nippin King while he is bouncing!

3. 💥 Repeat this 7 times to win!
