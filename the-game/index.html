The Game 💰 $0

Objective: Walk through the door into the weird lab!
Move: Click/Drag, or W A S D / Arrows
Action: SPACEBAR / E  |  Doors: F
Attack: Q  |  Weapons: 1 / 2 / 3

❓ HOW TO PLAY THE GAME

🔦 Explore in the Dark: Move with W A S D, Arrow Keys, or Click/Drag. Your flashlight cuts through the dark forest!
⚔️ Fight Bad Guys First (Press Q): Bad guys have weapons, bites, and claws and will fight back! Blast them with Q until their HP Bar hits 0 — they won't follow you until you beat all their health!
🧲 Capture & Cage: Once a bad guy's HP is 0 (💫 CAPTURE ME!), walk close to pick them up (even airborne Witch, Bat & Spider!), bring them to their Lab Pod or House Cage, and press SPACEBAR / E to throw them in!
🛒 Buy & Switch Weapons: Open ☰ Menu to buy the ⚡ Shock Blaster ($10), 🔥 Flame Sword ($20), and 🌈 Freeze Ray ($35). Press 1, 2, or 3 to switch weapons!
❤️ Watch Your Health: Step back inside the Lab or Abandoned House to safely heal your ❤️ Hero HP!
